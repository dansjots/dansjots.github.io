Remarks

Saw a discussion today mentioning Einstein’s proof of the Pythagorean theorem via subdividing areas.

In the process of working it out on paper in my own preferred notation, I realized that the proof could in fact be streamlined such that it could be included alongside the initial introduction of the theorem without adding much cognitive overhead.

This is actually the very first time that I’ve looked at any proofs of the theorem at all, so I was certain that something so simple must’ve already been conceived in the past, and sure enough it’s right among the very foremost proofs frequently included in most materials. However, for whatever reason, all of them uses an archaic form of notation that is very hard to parse mentally and, in my opinion, obfuscates thesimplicity of the proof by adding unnecessary cognitive overhead.

So that’s the motivation for writing it up in my preferred notation, mainly as a record for myself to refer back to in the future.
